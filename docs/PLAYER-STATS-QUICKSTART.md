# Player Stats - Quick Start Guide

## What's New?

A brand new **Player Stats** page has been added to AI Snap! Access your profile, view your achievements, and check your inventory all in one place.

## How to Access

### Option 1: Keyboard Shortcut

Press the **`I`** key while playing

### Option 2: HUD Button

Click the **👤** button in the top-right corner of the screen

## What You'll See

### Left Panel

#### 🎭 Avatar Preview

- Your 3D player avatar
- Rotates automatically
- Glowing cyan effect
- Real-time rendered

#### 📊 Quick Stats

- Photos taken
- Total score
- Best score
- Creatures discovered
- Play time

### Right Panel

#### 🏆 Achievements

Grid showing your creature photography progress:

- **Common** creatures (gray)
- **Uncommon** creatures (green)
- **Rare** creatures (blue)
- **Epic** creatures (purple)
- **Legendary** creatures (gold)

#### 🎒 Inventory

Your collected items:

- 📷 Camera (always equipped)
- 🖼️ Photos (your collection)
- 👑 Legendary Badge (if earned)
- 💎 Epic Badge (if earned)
- 💠 Rare Badge (if earned)

## Tips

1. **Track Progress**: Open the stats page regularly to see your improvement
2. **Rarity Hunting**: Try to photograph creatures of all rarity tiers
3. **Score Challenge**: Beat your best score by finding rare creatures
4. **Time Tracking**: See how long you've been exploring the park

## Keyboard Shortcuts

| Key   | Action              |
| ----- | ------------------- |
| `I`   | Toggle Player Stats |
| `ESC` | Close any open menu |
| `G`   | Open Photo Gallery  |
| `H`   | View All Shortcuts  |

## What's Tracked?

The system automatically tracks:

- ✅ Every photo you take
- ✅ Your cumulative score
- ✅ Unique creatures you've photographed
- ✅ Rarity distribution of your photos
- ✅ Total play time
- ✅ Your best single photo score
